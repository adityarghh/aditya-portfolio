A LITTLE MORE CONTEXT

Hey, I'm Aditya.

I'm usually interested in five different things at once. Occasionally, they even connect.

Music, design, psychology, politics, and whatever strange corner of the internet I've wandered into lately. I like pulling at a question until it turns into ten more questions. It's a terrible way to get through a to-do list, but a pretty good way to find ideas.

That curiosity tends to spill into what I make. I like playing with how things look and feel just as much as figuring out how they work. Sometimes it becomes a project; sometimes it's just a new folder called “experiment 2 final final”.

I'm still figuring out where all these interests lead. For now, I'm happy learning, making things, changing my mind, and seeing what sticks.
